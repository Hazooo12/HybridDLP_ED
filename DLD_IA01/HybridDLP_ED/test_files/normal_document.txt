This activity diagram illustrates the car return workflow at the design level, using separate swimlanes for Staff and System to clarify responsibilities.

The process starts when the staff accepts the returned vehicle and performs an inspection. The system records the inspection outcome. A decision node is used to determine whether the car has any issues such as damage or overuse.

If problems are identified, the system calculates penalty fees and updates the booking with additional charges, while marking it as awaiting payment. Otherwise, the booking is finalized without extra cost.

At the end of the process, the system updates the vehicle’s status to available, ensuring it can be rented again. The diagram highlights the control flow, decision-making, and coordination between human and system roles.
